Keyboard shortcuts & beta limits

Outside recording: ←/→, R/F, U/J, PageUp/PageDown navigate. During recording: WASD, OKL;, arrows translate; Q/I and E/P rotate. Shift gives 10 px / 1°. Ctrl+wheel zooms. Drag the viewer to pan outside crop mode. Finish stores a correction only; Apply changes the selected range once.

Designed for adjacent slices with similar content, roughly ±30° rotation and moderate displacement. Low-texture, repetitive, damaged or substantially different slices may fail. QC correlation is an image similarity indicator, not a scientific accuracy estimate. No TIFF, DICOM, 16-bit preservation, scale or deformation in this beta. Save transforms before closing or loading another stack.
