TIFF support & bit depth

Single-page, strip-based unsigned 8/16-bit grayscale or RGB TIFF; uncompressed, LZW or Deflate. Top-left orientation, interleaved samples, no alpha.

16-bit samples map from 0–65535 to 0–255. Display, registration and PNG/JPEG export are 8-bit; the original TIFF file is preserved. There is no automatic contrast stretch.

Multi-page, tiled, BigTIFF, signed/float, palette/CMYK, alpha, PackBits/JPEG/CCITT and other orientations are not supported. The TIFF decoder is bundled locally; no runtime CDN.

Original pixels preserved. Smaller images are center-padded.

Keyboard shortcuts & beta limits

Outside recording: ←/→, R/F, U/J, PageUp/PageDown navigate. During recording: WASD, OKL;, arrows translate; Q/I and E/P rotate. Shift gives 10 px / 1°. Over the viewer, wheel up/down selects the previous/next slice (one step per burst; pause 180 ms between bursts). Ctrl+wheel zooms. Wheel navigation pauses while recording, processing or editing a form field. Drag the viewer to pan outside crop mode. Finish stores a correction only; Apply changes the selected range once.

Designed for adjacent slices with similar content, roughly ±30° rotation and moderate displacement. Low-texture, repetitive, damaged or substantially different slices may fail. QC correlation is an image similarity indicator, not a scientific accuracy estimate. No DICOM, 16-bit export/preservation, scale or deformation in this beta. See step 1 for the supported TIFF subset. Save transforms before closing or loading another stack.
